### The Need

Writing smart contracts that supported interactions such as crafting or upgrading, required writing custom smart contracts. 

### The Solution

We crafted a smart contract factory along with a friendly front end that enabled users to create interactive NFT collections without writing any code. 

### The Stack

Typescript, Vue 3, Vercel Serverless Functions, PNPM and NX Monorepo, Hasura, GraphQL, Postgres, PWA
